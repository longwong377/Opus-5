# QUARANTINE — AI-generated character sheets, not show reference

**Do not model from anything in this folder.**

Nine files in `14-characters-and-uniforms/` and `15-races-and-makeup/` are AI-generated
character turnarounds, not photographs, screencaps or production art. They were identified by
inspection during the reference-mining pass of session 2q.

## Why they are out

They are not a record of anything. An AI turnaround is a *plausible* costume, not the costume
that was built — and the failure mode is silent, because the output is confident and clean.
Modelling an NPC wardrobe from these would put invented detail into the project labelled as
canon, which is exactly what `CLAUDE.md` rule 1 forbids.

Two of them are demonstrably wrong on facts we can check without leaving the reference set:

- **`Talia front back side grok.jpg`** puts Talia Winters in a **mustard/ochre** suit. Her
  actual costume is the pale grey Psi Corps suit with black gloves — see
  `14-characters-and-uniforms/Talia Winters in uniform.webp` and
  `talia-winters in gorgeous office.webp`, both genuine screencaps, in the same folder.
- **`pak'ma'ra front and back and side.jpg`** gives the pak'ma'ra a **two-lobed trunk**. The
  genuine stills in `15-races-and-makeup/` show a four-part mandible cluster.

If two of nine are checkably wrong, none of the nine can be trusted for the details we cannot
check.

## Identification — the signature

- Exactly **1280×720** (or a square/near-square crop), JPEG, clean **pure-white** background.
- A **turnaround layout**: head-and-shoulders inset plus front / side / back full-body, evenly
  lit, no shadow falloff, no film grain, no lens character.
- `psi corp uniform with logo.png` still carries its **generation prompt as burned-in labels**
  — "CLEAR HEAD-AND-SHOULDERS", "FULL-BODY Orthographic Reference".
- Faces are *near* the actor and not the actor.
- Most of them have `grok` in the filename, which is the uploader's own statement of
  provenance. Two do not (`Marcus Cole in ranger uniform side front back.jpg`,
  `pak'ma'ra front and back and side.jpg`) but share every other tell — **do not screen on the
  filename alone.**

## The same trap as folder 21, in a new costume

`21-QUARANTINE-animated-film/README.md` records the lesson: *resolution is not authority*.
This is that lesson again. `psi corp uniform with logo.png` is **2528×1696 and 4.6 MB — the
largest "uniform reference" in the whole set**, and it is worth nothing. The genuine EarthForce
reference is a 1025×713 fan vector diagram and a handful of soft screencaps.

## One caveat, recorded honestly

`more vorlon front side back grok.jpg` is the most convincing of the nine — it reads as
photography of a real costume on a stand, and Kosh's encounter suit has been exhibited. It is
quarantined on the filename's own provenance claim plus the turnaround layout, and because the
head geometry is inconsistent between views in a way a solid object cannot be. If a genuine
exhibition source for it ever surfaces, it should be reinstated at authority 2.

## What they may be used for

Nothing. Unlike folder 21 — which at least records a production that studied the original sets
— these have no chain of custody to the show at all.
