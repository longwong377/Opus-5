# QUARANTINE — 2023 animated film, not the original series

**Do not model from anything in this folder.**

These are frames from the 2023 animated feature, not from live-action Babylon 5. They were
identified by inspection after two of them were nearly used as design reference.

## Why they are out

Three independent reasons, any one of which is disqualifying:

1. **Wrong source.** The brief is "based only on the original design in the show". This is a
   different production with its own art direction, not a record of the original.
2. **Wrong era.** The uniforms are the later blue pattern, against a Season 2–3 era lock.
3. **Reinterpreted, not reproduced.** The C&C set, the concourse and the lift car are all
   redesigned — cleaner surfaces, different console layouts, different lighting language.
   Modelling from them would produce a station that is confidently wrong.

## Why this was easy to get wrong

**These are the highest-resolution interior references in the whole set** — around 2260×1180,
against genuine screencaps at 800×600 or lower. The temptation to prefer them is exactly
backwards: resolution is not authority. A crisp frame from the wrong production is worth less
than a blurry one from the right one.

## Identification

All eight share a signature: ~2260×1180, modern widescreen, flat cel shading, clean gradients,
no film grain or interlacing artifacts. Genuine Season 2–3 screencaps are 4:3-ish, soft, and
carry VHS/broadcast artifacts.

## What they may still be used for

Composition and spatial layout intuition only — the animated film was made with reference to
the original sets, so its *arrangement* of spaces is informative even where its *surfaces* are
not. Any such use must be logged in `canon/INVENTIONS.md`, because it is inference from a
secondary source, not canon.
