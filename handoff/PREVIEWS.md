# Previews

Every model in this bundle, rendered. The interior and drum frames come from the real engine with materials and lighting; the Starfury and crowd frames come from a flat-shaded software rasteriser that has **no materials at all**, so those two groups are silhouette only.

Grouped by whether they are worth taking, which is the honest answer to *is this worth importing*.

![all interiors](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/longwong377/Opus-5/claude/aaa-game-development-j6y2ml/handoff/previews/ALL_interiors_contact_sheet.jpg)

*All 35 interiors at a glance.*
